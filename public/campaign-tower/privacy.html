Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026 · Applies to the Campaign Tower app for iOS

Short version: Campaign Tower has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your game lives on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud. We don't collect personal data. Purchases go through Apple. If rewarded ads are enabled, the ad provider may process limited device data, described below.

1. Who we are

Campaign Tower is published by an independent developer. Contact: njvanderbyl@gmail.com.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not ask for or store your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any account credentials. The app does not contain analytics SDKs, and it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking. The device name you choose in iOS Settings is stored with your iCloud save so you can tell devices apart; it never leaves your iCloud account.

3. Game data on your device

Your tower, creatives, currencies, settings and progress are saved locally in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data unless it has been synced to iCloud (below).

4. iCloud sync (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, the app stores a copy of your save in your personal iCloud Key-Value store so progress can follow you between your own devices and survive a reinstall. This data is stored in your Apple account, is protected by Apple's iCloud security and privacy terms, and is not accessible to us. You can turn it off by signing out of iCloud or disabling iCloud Drive for the app in iOS Settings.

5. In-app purchases

Awards packs and the Agency Pass are sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple handles payment and keeps the transaction record. We receive no payment details. Refunds are handled by Apple.

6. Rewarded ads

The app offers optional rewarded video ads ("watch an ad to earn a bonus"). Ads are never shown without you tapping to start one. They are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC) and are non-personalised: we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission and we ask Google not to use your data for ad personalisation. To deliver and measure an ad, Google may process device information such as IP address, device model, operating system version and coarse location, under the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from ads. In the UK and EEA you will see a consent message before the first ad, and you can change your choice at any time from Shop → Settings → "Privacy options".

7. Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications (for example when a floor finishes building). These are generated on your device; no data leaves it.

8. Children

Campaign Tower is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on request — your data is on your device and in your iCloud, both under your control. If you have questions, email us and we'll help.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
